    26/27 AWAY KIT

    ST MIRREN FC's NEW 2026/27 AWAY KIT

    On sale now at the St Mirren Club Shop and online, our 2026/27 'Gold Standard' Macron away kit is a striking gold shirt embossed with Paisley pattern. It features a black buttoned collar and black trim on the sleeves. The shorts are black with a gold trim with gold socks.

    'The Gold Standard' celebrates our men's first team winning the Premier Sports Cup last season with final goalscorer Marcus Fraser modelling the kit. It also pays tribute to our men's U19s who won the CAS Elite U19s League Cup in May.

    26/27 HOME KIT

    ST MIRREN FC's NEW 2026/27 HOME KIT

    Our new home kit is based on our iconic 1987 Scottish Cup winning jersey.

    As we approach the 40th anniversary of our famous win over Dundee United at Hampden Park, we pay homage to the team that brought the trophy back to Paisley for the third time in our history.

    Working with our kit partners Macron, intricate details on the kit include the names of the side that won at Hampden in 1987, with a Scottish Cup trophy logo featured on the back of the shirt.
